Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

ChicMuse is a curated fashion discovery site. We collect very little data directly:

  • Newsletter signups: if you join our newsletter, we store your email address so we can send you fashion finds and updates.
  • Basic analytics: we may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are popular, such as page views and referrers. This data is aggregated and never sold.

What we don't collect

We don't run accounts, we don't process payments, and we don't store your name, address or payment details. When you click a "Shop Now" link you leave ChicMuse and shop on a partner retailer's site (such as Amazon) — their privacy policy applies there, not ours.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies needed for the site to work. Partner retailers may set their own cookies (for example, affiliate tracking cookies that credit ChicMuse for referrals) once you click through to their sites.

Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email.
  • You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work for browsing.
  • You can ask us to delete your newsletter email address by contacting us (see our Contact page).

Affiliate links

Some links on ChicMuse are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Read our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a revised date.
